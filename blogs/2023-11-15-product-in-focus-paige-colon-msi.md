Colon cancer is among one of the most common cancer diagnoses in men and women, with nearly 107,000 new cases estimated to arise this year in the United States alone. 1 Of those, 10-20% will exhibit microsatellite instability (MSI), an indicator of mismatch repair deficiency (dMMR), which is a distinct DNA damage repair deficit that directly influences a patient’s prognosis and treatment. 2 Yet today, many of those cases are being overlooked.
