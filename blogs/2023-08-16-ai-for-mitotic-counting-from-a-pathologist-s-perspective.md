A New Era of Breast Cancer Diagnosis Any pathologist who has diagnosed a breast cancer case knows all too well how arduous and inefficient mitotic counting can be, while at the same time how critical it is not only to diagnosis, but to deciding the treatment pathway that will follow. So, it is essential that pathologists get it right. Yet pathologists are only human, and humans can make mistakes – especially when trying to identify small cell structures under a microscope and manually quantify them.
