Following the recent launch of highly advanced artificial intelligence (AI) tool ChatGPT, global interest in better understanding the scope of AI’s power has swelled. Though AI for pathology is not a new phenomenon, that interest has extended into the medical space; pathologists are more curious than ever to learn how pathology AI works and how it can offer them value. To explore this, University Medical Center (UMC) Utrecht and Paige have joined forces to conduct research on the use of pathology AI in clinical practice.
