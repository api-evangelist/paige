A Practical Use Case of How Paige’s Foundation Model is Revolutionizing Pathology Cancer is extraordinarily complex. There are over 200 known types and subtypes, and even within a single subtype, individual tumors contain genetic diversity. Such exponential variety is what has made understanding cancer, and therefore treating it, a continuous challenge.
